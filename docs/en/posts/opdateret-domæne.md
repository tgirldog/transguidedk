# Updated Domain

Trans Guide has recently updated its domain from dktransguide.org to transguide.dk. This was done in the hope of making the site easier for Danes to find.

If you visit dktransguide.org, it will redirect to transguide.dk; however, this will not continue indefinitely, as I cannot afford to pay for two domains. The old domain will continue to redirect to the new one for some time, so that hopefully everyone becomes aware of this change.
