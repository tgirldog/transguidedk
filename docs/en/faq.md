# FAQ

**Is DIY hormone therapy safe?**

Generally, yes. There are always risks associated with medical treatment, and hormone therapy is no exception. If you do your research and avoid reckless behavior, it is just as safe as hormone therapy obtained through a clinic such as CKI or a private clinic.

**Is DIY hormone therapy illegal?**

Feminizing hormone therapy is not illegal, as you are legally permitted to possess and use estradiol and anti-androgens. Masculinizing hormone therapy, unfortunately, is a bit more complicated. Testosterone and other substances with androgenic effects are classified as doping agents; therefore, possessing them without a prescription is illegal.

**Blood tests are expensive. Do I really need them?**

Unfortunately, yes. It is important to monitor your hormone levels to ensure that your hormone therapy is effective and that no complications arise.

**Do I need progesterone?**

Many transfeminine individuals find that progesterone can have a positive feminizing effect and may significantly help with increasing breast size. That said, it is by no means necessary for a "successful transition." Some people find it helpful, while others notice no change at all. However, there is no harm in trying it; the worst-case scenario is simply that it does not work for you.
